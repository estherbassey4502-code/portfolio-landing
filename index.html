A little about me

Plot twist: I’m not always staring at a screen.

Hi, I’m Esther, a Product Designer turned Design Engineer with 4+ years of experience, mostly designing SaaS products and untangling complex workflows, multiple user roles, edge cases, and messy requirements into experiences that feel considerably simpler.

I’ve worked across Seven Apex Studio, OBST Solar Power Company, and Uzel, and I currently work at Gopaddi Technologies, designing and building products across travel, workforce management, AI, and business operations.

I love using storytelling in my design process—thinking about how each screen, interaction, and moment connects to create an experience that naturally carries the user from one point to the next. I’m also drawn to anything that lets me dip into my creativity, with colour as one of my favourite weapons for giving products personality and making them pop.

These days, I’m taking my work beyond Figma, using AI-assisted development to turn my designs into working experiences.

When the laptop closes... I’m probably singing, listening to music, exploring somewhere new, or picking up another hobby I absolutely didn’t need.
